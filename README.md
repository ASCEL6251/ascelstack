## About Ascelstack

Ascelstack is a single partner for everything a business runs on. Most small and growing companies hire a web agency, a marketing agency, an accountant for GST, a freelancer for the app and someone else for the CRM. None of these tools talk to each other, and the owner becomes the glue.

Ascelstack replaces that patchwork with one team and one connected stack. We build the systems, grow the business on top of them, and then automate the day-to-day work so it keeps moving without someone waiting at a desk.

## Business model

### Three layers, one stack

Every service we sell belongs to one of three layers. The logo, the Layered A, is built from the same three parts.

| Layer | What it covers | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Build** | The foundations the business runs on | Websites, web and mobile apps, SaaS, AI agents, cloud and IT |
| **Grow** | Getting found, chosen and paid | SEO, social, paid ads, branding, marketplaces, training, consulting |
| **Run** | Keeping daily operations moving | CRM, ERP, billing, payroll, restaurant systems, registrations and compliance |

### Who we serve

Services are packaged for the way each kind of business already works:

- **Restaurants and cafés.** Swiggy and Zomato onboarding, direct online ordering, POS and kitchen display, menus, reviews and FSSAI compliance.
- **E-commerce sellers.** Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho onboarding, an own store on Shopify or WooCommerce, catalogue SEO, inventory sync, GST and import–export registrations.
- **Startups and MSMEs.** Company registration and trademarks, product development, brand launch, lead generation and back-office systems.

### How an engagement runs

| Step | What happens | Typical timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Discover | Map how the business runs today: every hand-off, tool and spreadsheet | Week 1 |
| 2. Design | Brand, interface and workflow designs, signed off before any code is written | Weeks 1–2 |
| 3. Build | Software, sites and integrations shipped in short, demoable cycles | Weeks 2–6 |
| 4. Automate | Agents and workflows switched on step by step, with a human checkpoint at each one | Weeks 4–8 |
| 5. Operate | Ongoing monitoring, maintenance and improvement, with a weekly report | Ongoing |

### How we earn

- **Project work.** Fixed-scope builds such as websites, apps, stores, brand identities and registrations.
- **Ongoing services.** Monthly retainers for marketing, marketplace management, support and operations.
- **Automation and care.** Running, monitoring and improving the workflows and AI agents we put in place.

Because every layer comes from the same team, one engagement usually leads to the next: a registration leads to a website, the website to marketing, and the marketing to automating the orders it brings in.

### Why one stack

- **One team, one point of contact,** in place of five vendors.
- **Connected by default.** The store, CRM, invoices and reports share the same data.
- **Automation built in.** Repetitive work is handed to workflows and AI agents, and people handle the exceptions.
- **Compliance included,** so growth is never blocked by a missing licence or filing.
